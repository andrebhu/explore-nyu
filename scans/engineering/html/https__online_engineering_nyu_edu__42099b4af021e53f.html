About NYU Tandon Online

Learn more about NYU Tandon Online (anywhere, anytime learning), the online learning unit at NYU Tandon School of Engineering, one of the nation’s most respected schools of engineering, applied sciences, management and technology.

Accreditation and Awards

NYU Tandon Online ranked #7 in "Best Online Master's in Computer Information Technology Programs" for 2022 and has consecutively ranked in "Best Online Graduate Engineering Programs" by U.S. News & World Report.

People

NYU Tandon Online is a full-service unit that caters to the needs of online learning graduate students and faculty, partners with industry and societies, and handles the marketing and student services for the online graduate programs.

Future Students: Online Programs and Offerings

NYU Tandon Online offers fully online graduate degree options, courses, and certificate offerings. STEM related degree programs in the areas of Applied Urban Science and Informatics, Bioinformatics, Cybersecurity, Integrated Design & Media, and Management of Technology are available. There are also a variety of non-credit professional certificate options for anyone looking to build or enhance technical skills in the areas of data science, design, cyber security, programming & computer science, and more.
