Generative Design in Minecraft

The 2022 AI Settlement Generation Challenge is now underway!

Welcome to the Generative Design in Minecraft (GDMC) website. We are organizing the AI Settlement Generation Competition in Minecraft, where you can participate by writing an algorithm that makes an interesting settlement for a given Minecraft map. Do you like Minecraft and procedural content generation? Did you look at the villages in Minecraft and thought, I could write a better algorithm to produce settlements? Now is your chance to prove it.
The Settlement Generation Challenge is about writing an algorithm that can create a settlement for a given, unknown Minecraft map. The challenge is to produce an algorithm that is adaptive towards the provided map, creates a settlement that satisfies a range of functional requirements - but also looks good and evokes an interesting narrative. The goal is to basically produce an algorithm that can rival the state of the art of what humans can produce. Here is a short intro video explaining the competition:

If you want to know more details, such as how to participate, how we evaluate your entries, the rules, how to get started, etc, you should take a look at the GDMC Wiki . To reach out to us, or to join with our help community, that has plenty of information on how to get started with this, feel free to join our discord. You can find previous years entries, both their code, generated settlements and results in our "Results Section". Once again, congratulations to Tsukuba Team for winning the 2021 competition.

The submission deadline for the 2022 season is the 31st of May, and results will be announced at the Conference on Games (COG 2022) in August - and online. Submissions will open soon. Good luck!

Also, follow us on Twitter (@GenDesignMC) for updates and announcements.
