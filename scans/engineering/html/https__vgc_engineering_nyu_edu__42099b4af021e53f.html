ViDA Group

This is the wiki for the The Visualization and Data Analytics (ViDA) lab at New York University's Tandon School of Engineering. It contains resources primarily meant for our members and students. For additional information about our project, you can check our GitHub (https://vida-nyu.github.io) and project web sites:

https://www.vistrails.org/

http://www.birdvis.org/

https://reproduciblescience.org/

http://www.crowdlabs.org/

https://www.reprozip.org/

https://auctus.vida-nyu.org/

General Reminders

  • There's a link to recent changes in the Wiki. You might want to use that to see what's news.

You will need a user account to be able to edit and browse through this Wiki. Contact Huy or Rémi to get your account.

Once you have an account, simply log in, click on the edit tab above and start contributing!

If you've never used a Wikimedia wiki before, you might want to check out the How to edit a page webpage. Then practice your editing skills at the Sandbox!
